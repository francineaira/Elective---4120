## 🕊️ About the Activity

This activity focuses on creating a personal portfolio website using a website-building platform. It explores how digital tools can be used to develop an online space that presents personal information, skills, experiences, and creative elements through an organized and visually engaging design.

<br>

## 🌐 Activity Output

**Personal Portfolio Website**

The output is a one-page personal portfolio website created using **Base44**. Designed as a digital scrapbook, the website presents different aspects of my university experience, including my journey as a nursing student, student leadership experiences, personal reflections, and meaningful moments throughout university life.

The design uses a soft and minimalist aesthetic, incorporating warm neutral tones, blush pink accents, scrapbook-inspired elements, and personal photographs to create a personal and engaging presentation.

## ✧ Website Highlights

- **The Cover** – Introduces my personal portfolio and digital scrapbook.
- **A Little About Me** – Shares my personal background, university life, and experiences.
- **The Nursing Chapter** – Reflects on my journey as a nursing student, including clinical experiences and lessons learned.
- **Learning to Lead** – Highlights my student leadership experiences and the lessons that come with responsibility.
- **Little Things That Make Up University** – Celebrates the small moments, friendships, and experiences that made university life meaningful.
- **Beyond the Title** – Presents personal thoughts and reflections beyond academic and professional achievements.
- **Currently...** – Shares what I am working toward, learning, and figuring out at this point in my journey.
- **The Last Page** – Closes the digital scrapbook with a thank-you message and ways to connect.

<br>

## ✧ Skills and Concepts Applied

- **Website Development** – Using a website-building platform to create a personal portfolio.
- **Digital Design** – Applying visual elements to establish an organized and appealing website layout.
- **Personal Branding** – Presenting personal identity, experiences, and interests through a digital platform.
- **Content Organization** – Arranging information into sections for easier navigation and understanding.
- **Visual Storytelling** – Using photographs, written reflections, and design elements to communicate personal experiences.
- **Technology Integration** – Exploring how digital tools can simplify website creation and development.

## 🪷 Reflection

This activity provided an opportunity to explore website creation and the use of digital platforms in developing a personal online presence. Through creating my portfolio website, I learned how design, content organization, and technology work together to present information effectively.

More than simply showcasing personal information, the website became a space where I could document my university experiences, reflect on my growth, and share the moments that have shaped me as a student and an individual.

It reminded me that a personal portfolio does not always have to be a collection of achievements. It can also tell a story about the experiences, people, and lessons that make up one's journey.
