## 🕊️ About the Activity

This activity focuses on developing a personal visual identity through the thoughtful selection and application of colors and typography. It explores how visual elements can communicate personality, establish a consistent aesthetic, and create a recognizable personal brand.

## 🎨 Activity Output

**Personal Visual Identity**

The output showcases a personal visual identity developed through different design components, including a logo, color palette, typography, tagline, and resume header. These elements work together to establish a cohesive and consistent visual presentation.

## ✧ Components of the Visual Identity

- **Logo** – A visual representation of personal identity through a distinctive design.
- **Color Palette** – A selection of complementary colors that establishes the overall visual aesthetic.
- **Typography** – The chosen typefaces and font styles used to create a consistent and readable design.
- **Tagline** – A short statement that communicates personal identity and serves as a recognizable personal expression.
- **Resume Header** – A personalized header that applies the established visual identity to a professional document.

## 🪷 Reflection

This activity provided an opportunity to explore the role of color and typography in developing a personal visual identity. It emphasized the importance of consistency, thoughtful design choices, and the relationship between visual elements in creating a cohesive personal brand. Through this activity, I gained a better understanding of how design can communicate individuality while maintaining a professional and recognizable appearance.
