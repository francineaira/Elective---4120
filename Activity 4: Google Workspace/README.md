## Skills and Concepts Applied

- **File Storage:** Understanding how Google Drive stores digital files in one accessible location.
- **File Organization:** Organizing files and folders for easier navigation and retrieval.
- **File Accessibility:** Accessing stored files across compatible devices with an internet connection.
- **File Sharing:** Sharing files and folders with other users through links and access permissions.
- **Collaboration:** Understanding how Google Drive supports teamwork through shared files and collaborative work.

## Reflection

This activity helped me understand the importance of digital file management in academic and professional settings. Through exploring Google Drive, I learned how proper organization, accessibility, and file-sharing features can make tasks more efficient and support collaboration. These skills are especially useful in nursing, where organizing and accessing digital information is essential.
