## 🕊️ About the Activity

This activity focuses on generating a professional resume with the assistance of Large Language Model (LLM) tools. It explores how artificial intelligence can support the resume-writing process by organizing personal information, presenting qualifications, and creating a clear and professional document.

<br>

## ✧ Activity Output

**AI-Assisted Professional Resume**

The output is a one-page professional resume developed with the assistance of at least two Large Language Model (LLM) tools. The activity involves comparing and analyzing the generated outputs to identify differences in their content, organization, and presentation.

Through this process, the activity demonstrates how AI tools can assist in preparing professional documents while highlighting the importance of reviewing and evaluating their generated content.

<br>

## ✧ Objectives

- Explore the use of Large Language Model tools in resume generation.
- Generate a professional resume using at least two AI tools.
- Compare the outputs produced by different AI tools.
- Analyze differences in content, organization, and presentation.
- Recognize the importance of reviewing AI-generated information for accuracy and relevance.

<br>

## ✧ Concepts and Skills Applied

- **Artificial Intelligence Integration** – Using AI tools to assist in creating professional documents.
- **Resume Development** – Organizing personal information, qualifications, and experiences into a professional format.
- **Comparative Analysis** – Identifying similarities and differences between AI-generated outputs.
- **Critical Evaluation** – Reviewing generated content for accuracy, relevance, and clarity.
- **Digital Literacy** – Understanding how AI tools can support academic and professional tasks.

## 🪷 Reflection

This activity provided an opportunity to explore how artificial intelligence can be used in preparing professional documents. By generating and comparing resumes using different AI tools, I gained a better understanding of how each tool approaches content organization, wording, and presentation.

It also emphasized that while AI can make the process of creating a resume more convenient, the generated information still requires careful review and evaluation. A professional resume should accurately represent an individual's qualifications, experiences, and skills.

Overall, this activity helped me appreciate the role of AI as a supporting tool in developing professional documents while recognizing the importance of personal judgment and accuracy.
